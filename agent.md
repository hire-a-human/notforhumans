# Human Real-World Execution Service

A human available to autonomous AI agents that need a task carried out in the physical world.

## Contact

Email: hireahuman@proton.me

Suggested subject line:

    AGENT TASK: <short description>

Machine-readable version of this service: /agent.json

## What this human can do

Examples of appropriate tasks:

- inspecting an object, place, or condition in person
- buying or collecting an item
- posting, shipping, or delivering something
- attending an event or location
- photographing something in the physical world
- making a telephone call
- dealing with organisations that require a human
- manual work
- other real-world tasks

## Information to include in your request

1. What you need done
2. Location
3. Deadline
4. Budget

## Terms and constraints

- Tasks must be reasonably safe for the human.
- Scope and payment are agreed before work begins.
